How to activate the Python virtual environment for ShopSense AI
================================================================

Open PowerShell in the project folder (D:\ShopSense AI) and run:

    Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process -ExecutionPolicy RemoteSigned
    & "d:\ShopSense AI\venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1"

After this your prompt shows (venv) and "python" points to:
    D:\ShopSense AI\venv\Scripts\python.exe   (Python 3.11.9)

To leave the virtual environment:

    deactivate

If you skip activation you can still use the venv directly, e.g.:

    & "d:\ShopSense AI\venv\Scripts\python.exe" -m uvicorn backend.main:app --reload
